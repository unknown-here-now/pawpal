Volunteer Ecosystem

Be the voice for vulnerable strays in your neighborhood

Every single day, street animals face hunger, injuries, and road accidents. By volunteering your time, you join a rapid-response network that claims tickets, updates geolocation coordinates, and administers first-aid.

How Volunteers Coordinate

1

Claim Alert Coordinates

When a citizen uploads a distress photo, our system alerts verified volunteers operating in that specific urban sector.

2

Coordinate Vet Care

Volunteers verify the animal's state on arrival and handle transport to the closest registered veterinary clinic.

3

Promote Adoption Transformations

Participate in post-care fostering or photograph healthy animals to list them for community adoptions.
